# Documents

Keep passports, ID cards, insurance policies, contracts and other papers in your vault. Vault reads the pages on your
device, fills in what it finds, reminds you before a document runs out, and makes redacted copies to send to someone.
Pages are read on this device and encrypted before they're saved. Nothing about them leaves the device unencrypted.

## The library

Open **All Documents** in the sidebar's Documents section. The section's header counts the documents and says how many
are due for renewal.

- **Coming Up** lists documents whose reminder has come, with the time left.
- Then a section per collection (Identity and the others), with **Show all** when there are more.
- **Inbox** holds documents not filed yet, such as the ones you shared to Vault from another app. It shows in the
  sidebar only while it has something in it.

Each card shows the first page, whose document it is, the date it ends and a badge with the days left.

- **Filters:** **All**, **Coming Up** and **Recent**. On iPad and the Mac, chips also filter by person: **Everyone** or
  one person.
- **Search:** finds names, field values, tags and words on the pages themselves.
- **On iPad and the Mac**, switch between **Cards** and **List**. In List, select several documents to set their
  collection, whose they are, or tags for all of them at once.

The first time you open Documents, Vault adds the default collections, Identity among them.

## Add a document

Choose how to bring the pages in:

- **Scan** (iPad and Mac) or **Add** (iPhone) opens the camera to scan pages.
- **Import** picks PDFs or pictures from Files. You can pick several.
- **New Document…** (⌥⌘N) from the New menu.
- Drop PDFs or photos onto the library (iPad and Mac).
- From another app, open a PDF in Vault or share it to Vault (see below).
- **On the Mac**, **File ▸ Import from iPhone or iPad** takes a photo or scans pages with your iPhone or iPad.

The **Add Document** sheet opens and reads the pages. Then:

1. Check the pages. Drag a page's corners to crop it, pick **Color**, **Grey** or **Document**, or use **Remove Page**.
   The **+** in the strip adds more pages.
2. Check the kind under **Looks like**. Vault suggests one; a passport's machine-readable zone fills in its fields and
   checks them. Pick another kind from the menu if the suggestion is wrong.
3. Fill in or correct the **Title** and the fields. A number such as a passport number goes in a hidden field.
4. Pick the **Collection** and **Whose** it is. **Add Person** adds someone new.
5. Add **Tags**, set **Remind me** (how many months before it ends) and add **Notes**.
6. Click **Add Document**.

## Open a document

Select a document to see its details: the fields for its kind, hidden fields with **Show** and **Copy**, how long it's
valid, its pages, copies you shared, linked items and notes.

Open it to see its pages large (**Open** on iPad and Mac, a tap on iPhone):

- **Zoom** and **Fit**, **Rotate**, and the page strip to move between pages.
- On a passport page, Vault marks the machine-readable zone and says whether its check digits match.
- **Find on pages** (iPad and Mac) highlights the matching lines.
- **Back** or Esc returns to the library.

## Reminders

In a document's details, **Reminder** sets how many months before the end date it shows in **Coming Up** (1 to 24),
with the date it will show. Turn **Remind** off to skip it. For a passport, Vault reminds you that many countries want
6 months left when you arrive.

Reminders show in Vault's Coming Up. Vault doesn't send notifications.

## Edit, duplicate and add pages

- **Edit** (or **More ▸ Edit…**) opens the document's values. Change them and click **Save**.
- **More ▸ Duplicate** makes a copy and opens it for editing, for example for next year's policy.
- The **+** in an open document's page strip adds pages.

## Delete a document

Choose **More ▸ Delete…**, then **Delete**. Its pages are deleted on all your devices. This can't be undone.

## Share a redacted copy

**Share a Copy…** makes a PDF to send to someone, with the parts you choose taken out of the pictures, not just covered.

1. Type who it's **For**. It goes into the watermark.
2. Leave **Watermark** on to print it across every page.
3. Turn on **Hide** for the number and, on a passport, the **Machine-readable zone**. The preview blacks them out.
   Vault warns you if it can't find a hidden value on the pages.
4. Turn **Include this page** off for pages you don't want to send.
5. Click **Make PDF**, then **Share…** (or **Save PDF…** on the Mac).

Once it's sent, a copy can't be taken back. Vault keeps a note of who got which copy under **Copies Shared**.

## Share to Vault from other apps

On iPhone, iPad and the Mac, Vault appears in the share sheet for PDFs and pictures, up to 10 at a time.

1. In Photos, Files, Mail or Safari, choose Share, then Vault.
2. The **Add to Vault** sheet shows what you're adding. Tap **Add to Inbox**.

Pictures shared together become one document; each PDF becomes its own. They land in the **Inbox** the next time
Vault comes to the front, and Vault reads their pages then. Vault takes PDFs and pictures only, up to a size limit
the sheet names. Open Vault once on a device before sharing to it there.

You can also open a PDF or a picture in Vault from Files ("Open in"). It goes straight to Add Document.

## Collections and people

**Settings ▸ Documents** manages the lists Documents uses:

- **Collections:** **Edit…** a collection's name, symbol and colour, move it up or down with **More**, or **Delete…**
  it (not Identity); its documents go to the Inbox. **New…** makes one, and a tap on a suggested one adds it.
- **People:** **Add…** a person with a name and a colour, edit, reorder or **Delete…** them. Their documents stay, as
  no one's.
