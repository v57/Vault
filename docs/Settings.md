# Settings

- **On iPhone and iPad**, tap the gear in the sidebar's bar. On iPhone each pane is a row with its current value.
- **On the Mac**, choose **Vault ▸ Settings…** (⌘,).

## General

- **Ask for Touch ID or Face ID:** the app lock, on by default. See
  [Privacy and Security](Privacy-and-Security.md#the-app-lock).
- **Lock decrypted folders after:** the time the Decrypt sheet picks first: 15 min, 30 min, 1 hour or 4 hours.

## Documents

Your document collections and the people documents belong to. See
[Documents](Documents.md#collections-and-people).

## Sync

- **Use iCloud**, the same switch as in Privacy. See [Sync and Accounts](Sync-and-Accounts.md).
- **Other devices** shows when a device on an older version wrote records, or when this device waits for keys.
- **Replaced values:** passwords and codes replaced by a later change on another device.
- **Check remotes:** how often Vault checks the remotes your [synced folders](Folder-Sync.md) use while it's open.
  **In the background** (iPhone and iPad) lets the system check them now and then while Vault is closed.
- **Hub:** **Vault records through** each of your Hubs. See [Hub](Hub.md#sync-your-vault-through-a-hub).
  **Live changes from your Hub** isn't available yet.
- **Notify about conflicts:** comes in a later version; conflicts show in the folder until you choose.

## Recovery Key

Create, check, replace or turn off your recovery key, or recover with one. See
[Recovery Key and Backup](Recovery-Key-and-Backup.md).

## Backup

**Export Encrypted Backup…** and **Restore from Backup…**. See
[Recovery Key and Backup](Recovery-Key-and-Backup.md#encrypted-backup).

## AutoFill

Describes Vault's AutoFill extension. You turn AutoFill on in the system settings: see
[Logins and AutoFill](Logins-and-AutoFill.md#autofill).

## Hub

Your Hubs with their status, and **Open Hub**; or **Add Hub…** when there's none. See [Hub](Hub.md).

## Privacy

**Use iCloud**: one switch for every v57 app on this device. See
[Sync and Accounts](Sync-and-Accounts.md#turn-icloud-off).

## Accounts

Your accounts, your name and Apple Teams, and this device's name. See
[Sync and Accounts](Sync-and-Accounts.md#accounts).

## Devices (iPhone and iPad)

Your devices, with **Rename…**, **Lost Device…** and **Remove…**, and the devices you stopped trusting with
**Trust Again…**. See [Devices and People](Devices-and-People.md).

## Remote (Mac)

Let your other devices sign in to this Mac over SSH. See
[Devices and People](Devices-and-People.md#let-your-devices-sign-in-to-a-mac).

## Updates (Mac)

The Mac app updates from v57's GitHub Releases.

- **Check for updates automatically** is off until you turn it on. While it's off, Vault never checks by itself.
- **Check Now**, or **Vault ▸ Check for Updates…**, checks once.

When an update is found, Vault shows it in a window. Installing it replaces the app in place and relaunches Vault.

## About (iPhone and iPad)

Vault's version. On iPhone and iPad, Vault updates through the App Store.
